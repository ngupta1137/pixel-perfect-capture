# Pharmacy Advocate Script — Retail Refills
Document ID: KB-PHARM-2201
Owner: Pharmacy Ops | Plan year: 2026

## Early refill threshold
Members may request a retail refill when 75% of the previous supply days have been used, based on the fill date and days supply on the prior claim.

## Quantity limits
Retail fills for maintenance medications are limited to a 30-day supply unless the plan benefit display shows a different retail day-supply maximum.

## When the claim rejects for too-soon-to-refill
Explain that the plan enforces an early refill limit. Offer the next eligible fill date from the claim system if available. Do not override the reject.
